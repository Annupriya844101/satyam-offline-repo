Workspace settings

Data & access

Manage local data, roles, and backup/export options.

Important security limitation: this standalone offline build uses browser-side role switching, not server-enforced authentication. It is suitable for a local prototype or single-device workflow, but not for protecting confidential data from technically skilled users. For secure multi-user online access, connect Supabase or another backend with server-side authorization.
